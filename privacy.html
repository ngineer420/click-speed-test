Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

cpsboost.com ("we", "the site") provides a free browser-based click speed test. This page explains what data is and isn't collected when you use the site.

How the tool works

The click speed test runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your clicks, timer state, and results are calculated locally on your device and are never transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by this site. Your personal best CPS score is saved only in your browser's local storage (localStorage) on this device — it is not uploaded anywhere, and clearing your browser data or switching devices/browsers will reset it.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks, such as Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or generally at aboutads.info.

Analytics

cpsboost.com has no server-side application, no account system, and no analytics beacons. The site does not count page views, visitors, or sessions. Nobody here sees your click speed results, because the site never receives them.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to hello@goodbotbad.bot. The same address is the Contact link in the footer of every page on this site.
