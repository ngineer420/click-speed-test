Two fingers, one button

Butterfly clicking uses two fingers — usually index and middle — landing alternately on the same mouse button, so each finger only has to move at half the resulting click rate. That is the whole trick, and it is why butterfly clicking is easier to learn than jitter clicking and far easier to sustain: nothing has to be tensed, and each finger is working at a rate it can comfortably manage.

The catch is that alternating is a rhythm, and rhythms fall apart. What normally happens to a beginner is that one finger gradually takes over while the other rides along contributing nothing, and the score quietly becomes an ordinary one-finger score. From the outside — and from a plain CPS number — the two are indistinguishable.

So this page reads the gaps between your clicks rather than just counting them. Two fingers alternating leave a long-short-long ripple in the timing, because the return stroke of one finger and the strike of the other are never quite the same length. The alternation figure under your result is how often consecutive gaps flipped across your median gap: near fifty percent is what evenly-spaced tapping looks like, and sixty-five or above means the two-finger rhythm genuinely held.

Questions

Why does the test say my alternation did not hold?

Almost always because one finger took over. Slow down deliberately — butterfly at six clicks a second with both fingers really working is better practice than nine clicks a second with one finger doing eight of them. The number should climb as the rhythm stabilises.

Is butterfly clicking allowed?

More often than drag clicking and less often than normal clicking. Because it produces bursts with unusually regular timing, some anti-cheat systems flag it as an autoclicker, and plenty of Minecraft servers ban it outright for that reason. It is a physical technique, but being physical has never stopped a CPS cap.

Butterfly or jitter — which is faster?

Butterfly is usually faster to learn and more comfortable to sustain, and it holds up better over thirty and sixty second runs. Jitter has a higher ceiling in short bursts. Neither is worth an injury, and butterfly is by a distance the kinder of the two on your forearm.
